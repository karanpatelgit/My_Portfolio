Mission & Vision

What Inspire Kushinara stands for.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide rural learners — especially in Kushinagar and surrounding districts — access to quality, practical education in technology and mathematics, bridging the urban-rural digital divide.

🌟

Our Vision

A Kushinagar where every student, regardless of economic background, can access skill-based education that opens doors to careers in technology, data and modern professional fields.

Filling the Gap Between Theory and Practice

Most rural schools teach theory from textbooks. Inspire Kushinara adds the missing layer: hands-on, project-based learning that students can apply in the real world — even with basic devices and limited internet.

What We Offer

Domain-specific learning pathways designed for practical outcomes.

💻

Basic Computer Skills

MS Office, typing, email, internet safety and file management — essentials for entering today's workforce.

🐍

Python Programming

From variables to automation — beginner Python taught through real-world village-level problems like crop data and local business tracking.

📊

Data & Spreadsheet Literacy

Excel, Google Sheets and basic data analysis for students who want to work in finance, government or business.

📐

Mathematics Enrichment

Going beyond the U.P. Board syllabus — helping students see the beauty and application of mathematics in daily life and careers.

🎨

Graphic Design & Content Creation

Canva, social media content and basic video editing — empowering students to build digital presence and income.

🗣️

Spoken English & Communication

Practical spoken English skills focused on interview readiness, workplace communication and confidence-building.

How It Works

Simple, accessible, community-driven learning.

Step 1
Connect via WhatsApp or Lynk.id
Students and parents reach us through social media or our Lynk page to enquire about programmes.
Step 2
Free Orientation Session
A free introductory session — online or in-person — to understand the student's goals and recommend a pathway.
Step 3
Structured Learning Path
Personalised modules delivered at the learner's pace, combining video content, live sessions and practice tasks.
Step 4
Projects & Portfolio
Students complete real projects — not just theory — building a portfolio they can show employers or use in their community.
Step 5
Community & Mentorship
Alumni and active students help each other — a peer-learning network rooted in Kushinagar values.
