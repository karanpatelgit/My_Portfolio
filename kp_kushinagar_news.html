About the Channel

Why Karan Patel Kushinagar News exists.

Kushinagar is a historically significant district — home to the parinirvana site of Lord Buddha and a growing young population. Yet, local achievements, community stories and important development news often go unreported by mainstream media.

Karan Patel Kushinagar News was created to give the district a digital voice — celebrating local talent, reporting ground-level development honestly, and building a connected community across social platforms.

As someone born and raised near Kushinagar, I believe every story here deserves to be told — with accuracy, empathy and pride.
